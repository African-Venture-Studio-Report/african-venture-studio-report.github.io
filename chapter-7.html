Chapter Seven

Studio support tapers as companies mature, with stalled rounds still to be explained

The deal record shows where recorded financing stops but cannot show why. Practitioner interviews describe support with no formal graduation date, and most explanations for a stalled round remain untested until portfolio founders and external investors are interviewed.

In practitioner accounts, studio support tapers with no formal graduation point

Practitioner interviews describe a model with no cohorts and no graduation date (Exhibit 35), and Exhibit 36 draws the taper they describe. The report’s milestone, the first external round, is observable in the deal record, though studios do not necessarily treat it as the end of support.

The first external round therefore marks a financing event, and studio support continues after it. It needs no studio cooperation and matches a marker proposed in the interviews. The round may predate the studio’s involvement in a company it invested in, and the deal record does not date that involvement. Time to first round runs from launch, so it measures company age at that round. It does not measure the length of support.

Eleven possible bottlenecks, none yet tested with founders or external investors

Eleven mechanisms could stall a first round (Exhibit 37). They are hypotheses, unranked and not exhaustive, and only one finds indirect support in the deal data.

Where rounds stall therefore remains a set of testable hypotheses, and the one indirectly consistent with the deal data points to a shallow pool of later stage investors. Chapter 8 sets out what funders, studios, investors and policymakers can act on now, and what they should test first.
