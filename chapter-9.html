The report establishes how many studio companies reach a first external round, and the next question is why some studios do better. Its two strongest comparisons, higher funding rates at studios with their own funds and the follow-on advantage after a first round, could reflect what studios do or which companies they choose to back. Four company records would separate the two, led by each company’s build route and the studio’s stake (Exhibit 39).
